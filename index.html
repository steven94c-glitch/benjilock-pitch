Executive Summary

The Opportunity

BenjiLock invented the fingerprint padlock — a product category that previously did not exist. Validated by Shark Tank investment, national media coverage, and consumer adoption, the company is seeking private capital to fund a structured two-year relaunch across product development, wholesale distribution, and marketing.

Business

BenjiLock designs and sells biometric padlocks that replace keys with fingerprint recognition. Products span residential, travel, commercial, portable, and cycling applications. The technology fits a standard padlock form factor — familiar to consumers, with no app or connectivity required.

Thesis

The global padlock market is large and underserved by technology. BenjiLock holds first-mover advantage, recognizable brand IP, and Shark Tank-earned consumer trust. The relaunch plan is focused on wholesale channel build-out and product line expansion to capture shelf space and recurring revenue.

Founder & CEO

Robbie Cabral conceived BenjiLock at the gym, frustrated by the inability to open his own padlock. Named after his dog Benji, the company was built without a technical background — through persistence, iteration, and a willingness to pitch the idea to anyone who would listen. The result was a Shark Tank deal, a new product category, and a brand with lasting consumer recognition. Robbie leads all product strategy, investor relations, and brand development.
