Executive Summary

The Opportunity

BenjiLock invented the fingerprint padlock — a product category that previously did not exist. Validated by Shark Tank investment, national media coverage, and consumer adoption, the company is seeking private capital to fund a structured two-year relaunch across product development, wholesale distribution, and marketing.

Business

BenjiLock designs and sells biometric padlocks that replace keys with fingerprint recognition. Products span residential, travel, commercial, portable, and cycling applications. The technology fits a standard padlock form factor — familiar to consumers, with no app or connectivity required.

Thesis

The global padlock market is large and underserved by technology. BenjiLock holds first-mover advantage, recognizable brand IP, and Shark Tank-earned consumer trust. The relaunch plan is focused B2C first through Amazon and Walmart. We'll then drive additional growth through Wholesale and Licensing.

Robbie Cabral

Founder & CEO

Robbie Cabral is the Inventor, CEO, and Founder of BenjiLock, the award-winning biometric security company known for pioneering patented fingerprint hybrid technology. Born in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic and now based in Los Angeles, Robbie launched BenjiLock in 2014 with a vision to modernize personal security and reinvent an industry that had remained largely unchanged for more than a century.

Robbie's breakthrough moment came on ABC's Shark Tank, where he secured a deal with investor Kevin O'Leary — an opportunity that helped transform BenjiLock into a global brand. Since then, Robbie has led the company's expansion through major retail partnerships and international licensing agreements that bring biometric innovation to travel, fitness, home, and lifestyle products.

Because of Robbie's leadership and inventiveness, BenjiLock has earned over 50 industry awards, including five CES Innovation Awards, the Thomas Edison Award, recognition from the United States Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, and national distinction from the U.S. Chamber of Commerce as one of America's Top Small Businesses. Most recently, Robbie's BenjiLock Fingerprint Padlock was named one of TIME's Best Inventions of 2025, one of the most prestigious honors in technology and product design.
