The Problem

Keys Are a Relic of the Past

Billions of people carry physical keys to padlocks every day — keys that get lost, copied, and forgotten. Traditional locks haven't meaningfully evolved in a century.

  • Lost keys cost consumers millions in locksmith fees annually
  • Shared-key access creates exploitable security gaps
  • Forgotten combinations lock people out of their own property
  • No modern biometric alternative existed for padlocks — until now

The Solution

Your Fingerprint Is the Key

BenjiLock replaces the key entirely. Touch the sensor, the lock opens. No app, no wifi, no password. The familiar padlock form factor with modern biometric technology inside.

  • Instant fingerprint recognition — no fumbling required
  • Register multiple authorized users
  • Traditional backup mechanism included for peace of mind
  • TSA-approved and built from hardened stainless steel

Founder

Built by a Builder

BenjiLock was born from a real frustration and real determination — the kind of origin story that resonates from the pitch room to the retail shelf.

R

Robbie Cabral

Founder & CEO

Robbie Cabral conceived BenjiLock while struggling to open a padlock at the gym — a moment of everyday frustration that sparked an entirely new product category. He named the company after his dog Benji, who walked beside him every morning as he worked through the concept. With no engineering background, Robbie went from idea to Shark Tank deal through sheer persistence and vision. His mission: make biometric security as universal and accessible as the traditional lock it replaces.
