The Problem

Keys Are a Relic of the Past

Billions of people carry physical keys to padlocks every day — keys that get lost, copied, and forgotten. Traditional locks haven't meaningfully evolved in over a century.

  • Lost keys cost consumers millions in locksmith fees annually
  • Shared-key access creates security vulnerabilities
  • Forgotten combinations lock people out of their own property
  • Existing "smart" locks require wifi, batteries, or phones — padlocks had no modern alternative

The Solution

Your Fingerprint Is the Key

BenjiLock replaces the key entirely with your fingerprint — no app, no wifi, no password required. Just touch and open. Backed by a traditional lock body for universal trust.

  • Instant fingerprint recognition — no fumbling with keys
  • Multiple authorized users can be registered
  • Traditional backup mechanism for peace of mind
  • TSA-approved for travel; stainless steel for durability
